## The shape of it, in one paragraph

Everything that could be *fixed* was fixed and nearly every fix carries a test
that was watched failing. Everything that required *choosing* — the fixture,
CI, the golden suite, the test runner, all five documentation items — was
left. That is the wrong half to leave: the eleven undone items are what make
the twenty-one done ones durable, and **four of the last round's own regression
tests sit behind a bundle gate no machine without game assets can pass.** Three
new mechanisms also have a hole of exactly the shape they were built to close —
a check that covers the case it was written for and not its neighbour.

### How `N17`–`N20` were closed

Not by correcting the numbers — that is what the previous round did, and they
were wrong again within a week. By removing the second source:

* **[`STATUS.md`](STATUS.md) is generated** by `tools/status.py` and holds every
  countable fact: the tree, coverage, divergences, ratchets, the two interface
  seams. `status.py --check` is one of the checks, so a commit that moves a
  number and does not regenerate fails. No other document quotes one. The drift
  it replaced: 5,716 lines, 17 divergences, 412 named functions, 15 points of
  coverage, three `PlayerCommands` members.
* **`tools/verify_all.py` is the canonical list of checks**, one authored table
  with a sentence per check saying what only it can see, rendered into
  `STATUS.md` and printed by `--list`. It replaces five shell blocks, four of
  them stale. It counts **skips separately from passes**, which is the
  mechanism `N3` is a symptom of.
* **[`LESSONS.md`](LESSONS.md) is the one traps list**, `L1`–`L20`, cited by id
  from the three skills and `CLAUDE.md`. It replaces four lists that shared six
  entries in five wordings.
* **One order of work.** `PLAYER_ARCHITECTURE.md`'s table is the single ordered
  list; both review files contribute findings and the shape of each fix, and
  neither carries a competing plan. The self-contradiction `N17` found — one
  section saying step 23 closed `layers-are-systems`, another three sections
  later saying it was still at 1 — is gone with the numbers that caused it.
* `README.md` rewritten as the front door, with the canonical-source table; the
  Blender and glTF material moved to `EXPORTING.md`. `PLAN.md`'s descoping of
  behaviour replaced with what actually happened. `hod2lib/__init__.py`'s
  module list rewritten and **checked** by `verify_exporters.py`, in both
  directions, because a list of files is checkable.
